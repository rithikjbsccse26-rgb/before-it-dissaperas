About Before It Disappears

Before It Disappears is a digital memory archive created to preserve the stories connected to different places.

Every place has something special about it. It could be a childhood home, a school corridor, a favourite street or a holiday destination.

This website allows people to share their experiences through photographs and stories.

Memories are stored using JavaScript Local Storage, allowing them to remain available in the same browser after refreshing the page.
